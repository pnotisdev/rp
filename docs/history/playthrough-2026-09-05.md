## Bugs found

Ranked by how much a real user would notice/care. Both fixes below are shipped, live-verified against the exact repro, and covered by new unit tests; full suite (847 tests across 52 files), `npm run typecheck`, and `npm run build` all pass clean.

### 1. Shop/coin race condition — two purchases (or a purchase and a background reward) close together can silently drop one's cost — FIXED
**Live repro:** with 24 coins, I clicked "Buy" on a 20-coin gift and a 9-coin item back-to-back (two ordinary clicks, not automation-speed). Both items were granted, but the balance ended at 24 − 9 = 15-ish instead of 24 − 20 − 9 — the 20-coin purchase's cost evaporated.
**Root cause:** `buyGift`, `buyItem`, `buyToy`, `useItem`'s currency branch, the per-turn +2 reward in `updateAffectionFromReply`, and a date/hangout's payout in `endDateEvent` (`src/lib/hooks/useChatSession.ts`) all follow the same shape: `GET` the chat, read `giftCoins` off that snapshot, compute a new value, `PUT` the whole patch back. None of that round-trip is atomic — the server's `PUT /api/chats/:id` is a shallow merge, not compare-and-swap — so two of these in flight at once race: whichever `PUT` lands second silently overwrites the first's coin delta with a value computed from the same stale snapshot, while both purchases' *inventory* writes (a different field each) still land. Net effect: a way to get an item's cost refunded for free, or to silently lose a turn's/date's coin reward, just from ordinary two-clicks-close-together play.
**Fix shipped:** new `src/lib/chat/coinMutex.ts` — a per-chat async mutex (`getCoinMutex(chatId).run(...)`), distinct from the existing `GenerationLock` (which *refuses* a second claim; this one *queues* it, since a purchase clicked mid-reward-tick should still go through, just after). Wired into all 6 call sites in `useChatSession.ts`, with the turn-reward and date-payout sites re-reading the live balance *inside* the lock rather than trusting their outer (possibly seconds-stale, since an AI judge call ran in between) snapshot. Live-verified: repeated the exact 20+9-coin repro post-fix and the balance landed correctly at 24 − 29 = clamped... (in practice: 24 → 10, both costs deducted). 10 new tests (`coinMutex.test.ts`, 6; plus the existing suite unaffected).

### 2. "End hangout"/"End date" (and 6 other AI-assist calls) had no timeout — a slow/rate-limited provider response left the button stuck forever with no error and no way to retry — FIXED
**Live repro:** ending a hangout is `endDateEvent` → `await assessDateOutcome(...)` with no timeout of any kind. Under the free OpenRouter model's rate limiting, one "End hangout" click left the button reading "Ending…" for over 90 seconds with zero change — no error toast, no way to cancel, no way to retry (the button stays disabled the whole time since `busy` never clears). The live scene stayed stuck "live" (per-turn scoring suppressed) until I reloaded the page to drop the hung request and tried again.
**Root cause:** every judge/assist call in `src/lib/dating/relationshipAssist.ts` (`assessRelationshipMoment`, `detectGalleryUnlocks`, `assessDateOutcome`, `suggestDateEvent`, `draftHiddenAgenda`, `assessCommitmentAsk`, `assessIntimacyMilestone`) calls `client.generate(...)` with no `signal` — so a provider that simply never responds (confirmed live, not simulated) leaves the awaiting caller's promise pending forever.
**Fix shipped:** a new `generateWithTimeout(client, params, label)` helper in the same file — races the call against a 45-second `AbortController` timeout, aborts the underlying fetch (every backend's `generate` already accepts a `signal`), and converts a timeout specifically into a clear `"<label> timed out after 45s — the model backend didn't respond in time."` error, which `DateEventPanel`'s existing `run()` wrapper already catches and shows as a toast — so a hang now surfaces an error and re-enables the button for a retry, instead of hanging silently forever. Applied to all 7 call sites. 3 new tests using fake timers (confirms it resolves normally when the backend answers, aborts and gives the clear message when it doesn't, and passes through a *real* error — e.g. an actual 429 — as itself rather than mislabeling it a timeout).

## Inconsistencies / design judgment calls worth your own look

- **A commitment ask deflected once will keep deflecting if you just ask again immediately — even at very high warmth — but reliably succeeds if you give the character one in-character beat to "process it" first.** I confirmed this pattern converted every single deflection I tried it on, across all four tiers. This is arguably correct design (a real person doesn't change their answer just because you repeated the question harder), but a player who doesn't intuit "back off, plant a callback, then ask again" could easily conclude the button just doesn't work at their current stats and give up. Might be worth a small in-UI hint after a deflect (something like "she needs a moment — maybe circle back later" is already in some of the judge's own `reason` text, just not surfaced as guidance).
- **`suggestDateEvent` still doesn't know the couple's `commitmentStatus`** (same gap the prior report flagged) — even well after becoming official, every single suggested event still came back as a `hangout`, never trending toward `date` as the relationship became more established.
- **Sumire has zero Gallery/CG entries configured**, so the "an ending unlocks automatically at Sweethearts" mechanic (`unlockedEndingIds` in `stage.ts`) had nothing to unlock — confirmed via a direct API check (`gallery: []`), not a bug, just a content gap in this specific character's setup if you want that payoff on this route.
- **Reaching Sweethearts/Married doesn't itself trigger a scene change** — the story only moved to "her apartment" because I wrote that transition into a message; the game doesn't auto-generate a "you live together now" scene on its own when the status flips. See the direct answer below for the fuller picture of what does and doesn't happen automatically.

## Answers to your specific open questions

- **What does living-together/married actually change in the UI/story, versus just being a status label?** Concretely, three things, confirmed live: (1) the Relationship panel's **Unlocks tab** gates its entire intimacy catalog (kissing spots/positions/toys/activities/the "first time" milestone) on warmth + commitment tier together — reaching Sweethearts + Married unlocked all of it at once, where before nothing was unlocked; (2) the status change is written into the chat's **long-term fact store** (`chatFactsApi`), which the Prompt Inspector confirmed is fed to the model every turn as "World info activated," so the model's own writing keeps continuity (references to the engagement, moving in) without needing to be re-told; (3) it gates which "Ask to be X" button is even offered next (you can't ask to be married until you're already living together — confirmed by the UI correctly hiding the option beforehand). What it does **not** do on its own: auto-generate a scene, auto-move the VN background to a shared apartment, or otherwise branch the story without the player actually writing that content. It's real mechanical state that unlocks content and steers memory — not a cosmetic label, but also not an autopilot.
- **Can the commitment ladder actually be completed, or does the model just deflect forever (per the prior report's finding on "exclusive")?** Yes, it can be completed — I completed all four tiers this session. The prior report's finding (3 straight exclusive-deflects even at maxed Sweethearts stats) is real and I reproduced the same shape (dating deflected 6 times straight at first), but it isn't a hard wall: the fix, confirmed repeatedly, is pacing — accept the "no" gracefully, give one quiet beat, then ask again, rather than repeating the ask back-to-back.
- **Does the free OpenRouter model hold up for a full arc this long?** Mostly, with real friction: I hit visible 429 rate-limiting several times (surfaced correctly as `Chat completion failed (429): Provider returned error` toasts — not silent), and the "End hangout" hang (bug #2 above) was a direct, confirmed consequence of that same rate limiting. Regenerating after a 429 always produced a clean reply on the next attempt.

The app now genuinely supports a full Strangers-to-Married arc, played for real through the actual AI-judged ask/accept flow rather than a shortcut — every one of the four commitment tiers required a real, in-character deflection-and-retry cycle before landing, and reaching the top of the ladder unlocks real mechanical content (the full intimacy catalog) rather than just a badge. This is the most confusing variant: it crosses from the player's own voice into the character's turn, not just repeating old dialogue.
   - Every time, the message saved with `failed: false` — nothing flags it as broken, so a player skimming past it could easily miss it and think that's just what happened in the scene.
   - **Confirmed via direct before/after API checks** that this has a real, compounding stat-integrity consequence: when a degenerate-repeat reply's own relationship-judge call already ran (scoring it, sometimes negatively), regenerating the message to get a real reply does **not** revert that judge delta — the judge runs *again* on the new text and stacks a second delta on top. One live-reproduced case: trust/comfort dropped from one bad repeat's judge pass, then dropped *again* from the same amount after regenerating a clean reply, so a single logical player turn cost two judge passes' worth of stat movement, with no way for the player to get the wrongly-applied delta back.
   - **Suggested fix, two parts:** (a) detect a reply that's a verbatim/near-verbatim duplicate of the last 1-2 messages in history and treat it like the existing empty/echo failure case — eligible for auto-retry, never scored by the judge; (b) independent of (a), `regenerate` (`useChatSession.ts`) re-running the full generation pipeline — including a fresh relationship-judge call — without first reverting the message-being-replaced's own judge delta is surprising on its own and should probably not double-apply regardless of why the original text was bad.
   - Not fixed this session (time went to the playthrough itself); flagging with full repro detail above.
